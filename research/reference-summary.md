## What the references agree on (how the service typically runs)

- **Service model.** "Fully managed" vending is the norm: the operator supplies, installs, stocks,
  cleans, maintains and repairs (Exact Vending, Best Vending, Vending Sense).
- **Commercial models.** Free-on-loan or revenue share (costs funded from product sales), lease or
  rental, or outright purchase (Brodericks). *AC Action's model is to be confirmed, so the site makes
  no cost claim.*
- **Warehouses.**
  - Breaks come in waves around shift changes.
  - Reliability matters most before night shifts.
  - Machines should go in a clean, accessible staff area, not on a loading route.
  - Combination snack and drink machines suit around 20–80 regular users (easyVend).
- **Offices.**
  - Demand is predictable.
  - The key factors are capacity, reliability and the right product mix (Westomatic).
- **Payments.** Contactless and card payments are expected; some sites still want cash (easyVend, Exact).
  *To be confirmed for AC Action.*
- **CTA language.** "Get in touch", "Get a quote", "Enquire now". The site uses "Discuss your site",
  in line with the brief's wording ("encourage prospective customers to discuss their requirements").
